| Marketplace product image suites | Complete | Signed-in customers upload one to three product angles, choose Amazon、Instagram Shop、Shopee、Lazada Thailand、Shopee Thailand、Facebook Shop or Google Merchant Center, review an AI-drafted and fully editable product brief, choose brand styling and select the exact output plan before generating and saving a project-aware suite (main, complete set, verified feature, lifestyle). The main remains pure-white catalog imagery; studio uses a non-white three-quarter angle, detail uses a close crop of a visible product element, and lifestyle uses a non-white confirmed-use setting. When lifestyle is selected, merchants can choose automatic, home, office or outdoor context; that preference affects only the lifestyle role and remains limited by confirmed product use. Completed account-saved cards accept a guarded per-image redesign instruction and regenerate just that role from the owned source at one image credit, while keeping product facts and SKU identity fixed. Merchants can also save/reapply/delete named workflow templates for platform, roles, lifestyle style, project and brand settings; templates never store source images, SKU, briefs, listing copy or outputs. Local reference checks flag low resolution, compression, low center/background contrast and visually busy borders before drafting, without blocking the customer. Each asset tries GPT Image 2 then one Gemini fallback using the same authenticated reference image and guardrailed prompt. Supplementary outputs that are near-white like a main image or too similar to a previous output are automatically regenerated once with a role-specific composition correction without a second customer image credit; unresolved cards disclose the concern and offer a single-image composition retry. A live role-by-role elapsed-time estimate is shown during creation. When only some roles fail, completed results stay available and the customer can retry the single failed role using the owned saved source (no duplicate source upload/storage); when every provider attempt fails, incomplete roles do not consume prepaid credits. Managed-storage, image-service and Google-export URL construction now rejects malformed configuration or URLs safely; pre-generation reference preparation returns a clear re-upload/retry message rather than a raw parser exception, with no usage entry or credit debit. Credits equal the number of selected images, and each result remains subject to SKU and platform review. |

The pipeline fails closed rather than returning an untagged Google asset. |
| AI product-brief review | Complete | A vision model drafts a traditional-Chinese product name, description, visible facts, supplementary-image callouts, use contexts and verification questions from one to three product angles. Unverified claims, dimensions, capacities, package counts, certifications and specifications are held as questions rather than used as facts. |
| Marketplace brand, project and plan controls | Complete | Merchants can select individual output cards before processing, create and manage multiple named account brand presets with an accent colour, typography direction and optional Logo, and assign a preferred preset to each project. The selected project preset overrides the account default; merchants can still use a one-off style. New projects support optional client name and concise internal description in both the suite and photo-library workflows; those labels make selectors clearer, but are never used in AI prompts or listing copy. The suite dialog also has a manual inline 「新增並選取」 project field, so a merchant can create a named project and immediately attach SKU data and generated images without leaving the workflow. Logo references are passed only to eligible supplementary images, never to catalog main images or Google Merchant Center assets. |
| Thailand listing workspace | Complete | Lazada Thailand and Shopee Thailand generate Thai listing titles and bullets from the merchant-confirmed brief. Lazada has a 255-character title counter; Shopee Thailand uses a 120-character title and 3,000-character description working target because official limits vary by market and seller. Both provide Thai description, leaf-category, brand, variation, category-attribute and package-logistics fields, while Lazada also includes box contents. CSV/XLSX exports are starting templates that require final Seller Center category and shop validation. |
| Account identity, security and administration | Complete | Each account can upload a PNG/JPG/WEBP avatar (maximum 2 MB, re-encoded to a bounded WebP in managed storage), select an Iwantphoto-only display name, review the latest 20 sign-ins/sign-outs/profile/avatar events, and securely switch to a real account. The protected `/admin` overview is visible only to `admin` roles and provides aggregated account, plan, image-storage and recent security-activity data without exposing customer image contents. An administrator may select Starter, Pro or Business only for their own test entitlement; it changes their personal limits and UI without altering any customer, Stripe subscription, payment status or billing. While that test entitlement is active, every route shows an amber test-mode banner with the effective and true plan plus a one-tap restore action. |
| Long-running marketplace suite reliability | Complete | Investigation of the reported final-image failure found one current Amazon suite with only main, studio and detail stored, while a repeat source completed all four roles. Each primary source and eligible brand logo is re-signed immediately before every role and any automatic composition correction attempt, so a late role never inherits an expired signed URL. The mobile workflow now submits one short request per selected role rather than holding one multi-minute four-image connection: every finished role is saved and displayed immediately, subsequent roles reuse the owned source, and a failed role does not prevent the remaining roles from running. The estimator is driven by actual completed role requests, never loops backward from 96%, explicitly identifies itself as an estimate, and switches to an honest long-running message after the expected duration. New results automatically scroll into view. Failed roles remain isolated, retriable, and charge only completed outputs. |
| Marketplace WhatsApp forwarding | Complete | Every completed Marketplace Suite result card offers a green WhatsApp action beside Download. The action shows a preparation state to prevent duplicate taps and has a safe download fallback with clear WhatsApp instructions where file sharing is unsupported or cancelled. |

Browsers that cannot share a multi-file payload use the existing ordered download fallback and explain how to select the downloaded images in WhatsApp. |
| Outdoor lifestyle scene fidelity | Complete | The selected Outdoor lifestyle style now requires a literal outdoor, natural-daylight setting rather than allowing an indoor or near-window substitute. Prompts require a plausible outdoor surface plus at least two visible environmental cues, while prohibiting seamless, isolated or near-white treatment. The composition check now treats light-neutral backgrounds and white outer edges as white-background failures for lifestyle images, uses a lower 60% threshold, and carries the merchant's Outdoor choice into its no-extra-credit automatic correction prompt. |

After the fast white/duplicate check passes, the lifestyle image is also visually verified for a recognisable setting, selected-style match and absence of studio/isolated treatment. A failure triggers the existing single no-extra-credit automatic rebuild; an unavailable verifier leaves the completed image intact rather than blocking delivery. |

Manual scene choices and saved workflow settings stay authoritative; the recommendation is not stored as a product specification or used as permission to invent a use case. |

Only the final choice travels to the image prompt as non-sold-environment art direction; it never becomes evidence of a product use, performance, accessory or product claim. Mismatched or tampered scene/style payloads are ignored server-side. |
